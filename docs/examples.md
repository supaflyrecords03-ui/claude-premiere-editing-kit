# Example prompts

## Cut-downs
- "Make 15s and 6s versions of this 30s ad. Keep the product shot and the end card."
- "This video is 1:20, the platform limit is 60s. Tighten it without touching the music."

## Revision rounds
- "Client notes for v3 are below. Turn them into a checklist first, then do them on a copy."
- "Which notes from round 2 are still open?"

## Reviews
- "Go through the sequence and leave FIX or CHECK markers on anything that looks wrong."
- "Check the timeline for gaps, disabled clips and audio without picture."

## Handover
- "Summarize what changed between v2 and v3 for the account manager."
